## Summary
Frank explores ways to make his open-source AzUrlShortener more budget-friendly by transitioning from Azure Container Apps to a split-resource architecture using Azure Functions and App Services. During the stream, he experiments with new dev tools like Kepler and the AZD CLI to customize deployment templates, all while navigating some typical "live coding" keyboard hurdles. The session is a classic BehindMyCloud vibe—full of community interaction, tool testing, and the honest reality of learning new tech even after 20 years in the industry.
