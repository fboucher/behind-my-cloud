Frank exploring cost-efficient ways to host his URL shortener, weighing options between Azure Container Apps, Azure Functions, and App Service sidecars. Between technical discussions on "sidecar" patterns and infrastructure as code, the community caught up on vacation stories and tested out some fun new interactive stream games.
