## Summary
In this high-energy stream, Frank introduces his new VS Code extension, **RainbowSQL**, which helps developers visualize long SQL insert statements by color-coding columns and their corresponding values. He also dives into the world of **Microsoft Entra** to secure his URL shortener's admin page, experimenting with identity providers and external user invites while managing his upgraded "Cloud Bot" (CB) running on a local **Ollama** instance. The vibe was interactive and playful, featuring medieval-themed stream effects, a successful hunt for a digital shovel, and plenty of "epic" landing games with the community.
