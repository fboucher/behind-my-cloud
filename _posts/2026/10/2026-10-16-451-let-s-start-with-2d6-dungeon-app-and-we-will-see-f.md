## Summary
Frank kicked off the stream by fixing a navigation bug in his 2D6 Dungeon App, working with a PR generated by an AI assistant to streamline how rooms are added to the map. Between testing the updated game mechanics and fighting off a cultist, the chat had a great time discussing everything from Linux distros and the new Google Pixel to an Easter egg discovered in GitHub Copilot. It was a classic "co-worker" vibe session that mixed deep-dive coding with community chat, even if his local AI model, Gemma, was a bit too strict on the rules!
