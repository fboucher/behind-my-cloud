## Summary
This stream was all about doors — literally! We added multiple door types (archway, wooden, reinforced, and more) to the 2D6 Dungeon map, implemented lock detection, and even started sketching out the lockpicking mechanic. We also had fun with multi-agent coding: Kimmy wrote the code while Anti-Gravity (Gemini) helped brainstorm the design. Frank also shared his early experiments with Hermes Agent for automating show notes. Oh, and Groverus took the win with a solid 74.48 score!
