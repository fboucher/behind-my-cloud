## Summary
In this 420th stream and Twitch affiliate anniversary, Frank continued building out the 2D6 Dungeon companion app, focusing on adding variety to the dungeon's doors beyond just simple archways. Using a mix of AI agents like OpenCode (with the Kimmy model) and Gemini to brainstorm and draft the logic, he successfully implemented multiple door types and a handy new map legend. The session had a highly productive and celebratory vibe, despite a slightly late start due to some exciting real-life updates!
