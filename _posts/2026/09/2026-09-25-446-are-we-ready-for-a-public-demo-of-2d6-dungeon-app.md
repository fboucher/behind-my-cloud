## Summary
Frank navigates a lingering cold and some container-slowness to tackle the 2D6 Dungeon companion app, focusing on polishing it for a potential public demo. Using a mix of Claude and the Kepler/GitKraken AI tools, he fixes a bug with health potion consumption and improves the documentation UI to make the app more standalone. The session features a lot of great community chat about everything from VS Code extensions like Rainbow CSV to gaming recommendations like Dungeon Crawler Carl and Underkeeper.
