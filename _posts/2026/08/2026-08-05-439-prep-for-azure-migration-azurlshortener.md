## Summary
Frank kicked off Stream #439 by showcasing his new "shout-out" feature for Cloudbot (CB), which uses local AI to research streamers' past videos and craft personalized intros. The session was a fun, high-energy mix of community interaction and deep-diving into the AzUrlShortener migration, where Frank wrestled with moving from expensive Azure Container Apps to a more cost-effective Azure Functions setup. While he hit some hurdles with how Azure Developer CLI (azd) handles Aspire projects, he mapped out a plan to tackle the Bicep infrastructure files in the next stream to get those costs down.
